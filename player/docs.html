Running in the car

A custom show runs on any supported model: the same file plays on Model S, 3, X, Y and Cybertruck. Each car uses only what it has.

Supported vehicles

Model S (2021+), Model 3, Model X (2021+), Model Y and Cybertruck, on software v11.0 (2021.44.25) or newer. Multiple shows on one drive need 2023.44.25 or newer.

Starting a show

Plug the drive into a front, USB-C or glovebox USB port and wait a few seconds. In Toybox, choose Light Show, then Schedule Show, and pick your show from the list.

If it doesn't show up

If the popup says "Light Show" instead of "Custom Light Show", the drive doesn't meet the format or folder requirements. If the files are there but something is wrong with them, the car shows an error message.

USB drive

The car only reads shows from a drive set up exactly like this.

The drive must not have a TeslaCam folder at its root. If you also use it for the dashcam, keep shows on a separate drive.
LightShow folder

At the root of the drive, named exactly LightShow (case sensitive).

  • A ramp only stretches how long a light takes to turn on or off. You can't hold a light at half brightness, except on Cybertruck channels with full brightness control.
  • To make sure a light reaches 0% or 100%, the effect must last at least 50 ms longer than the ramp.
  • On cars without full brightness control, anything above 50% means on.
  • Channel 4 sets the ramp duration for channels 4, 5 and 6, so sometimes you need an effect on channel 4 even when it isn't lit.
  • The minimum visible on-time is 15 ms. For a pleasant effect, keep lights on or off for at least 100 ms.

Model details

Model 3 and Y: channels 4–6

One lamp per side, lit when any of them is on. Channel 4 sets the ramp duration.

Model 3 and Y: park lights and side markers

They work together and light up on any of the 4 channels (left/right). For a flash, all four need to be off between flashes.

Model S: park lights and side markers

They work together, but separately for left and right.

Side markers

Only on North American cars.

Front fog and park lights

Missing on Model 3 Standard Range Plus. No other light takes their place.

Rear fog light

On cars outside North America, and on North American Model X.

Model 3 built before October 2020

Left and right tail lights and the license plate light work together. The license plate channel has no effect.

Cybertruck: 200 channels

200-channel shows add the Cybertruck light bars and the interior RGB lights. Older 48-channel shows are remapped automatically.

Remapping for older shows

  • Reverse lights follow the left and right tail lights.
  • Rear side markers follow the side repeaters.
  • The powered frunk follows the liftgate channel, and the frunk light follows the park lights.
  • Bed lights follow the reverse light channel.
  • Rear turn signals are disabled.

How Studio generates

The generator analyzes the song (tempo, beats, bars, kick, snare, hi-hats, energy) and writes a 48-channel .fseq, validated against the rules above.

Sections

Each bar gets a level: quiet parts make the headlights "breathe" with slow ramps, verses pulse and alternate the main beams, the two bars before a chorus chase left to right, and choruses sweep the headlights like a matrix with park lights on the off-beat.

Drums

Kicks flash the brake light, snares the fog lights (or every headlight in a chorus), hi-hats the turn signals. Hits are snapped to the beat grid. Every flash lasts at least 120 ms.

Moving parts

Mirrors fold on the strongest hits (up to 20 commands), the charge port opens with a rainbow LED in the longest chorus (3 commands). With "everything that moves", windows dance in the three longest choruses (about 24 s in total) and close at the end; Model Y, S, X and Cybertruck also open the liftgate or frunk, and Model S presents its door handles. Doors never move, for safety.
